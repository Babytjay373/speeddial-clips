Questions

Before you commit

How fast is turnaround?

48 hours for up to five clips. Five days for ten or more. If you need it faster, say so and I'll tell you honestly whether I can hit it.

What do you need from me?

The source video file, your logo if you have one, and the handle you want tagged. That's it — I handle the rest.

Do you guarantee views?

No, and neither can anyone else. What I guarantee is the craft: the hook is written deliberately, the captions are accurate, and the specs are right so the platform doesn't suppress it. Performance depends on your content and your audience.

Can you match my existing style?

Yes. Send a clip you like and I'll match the caption style, font, placement and pacing.

What if I don't like the first clip?

You don't pay for it. Starting with one is the whole point — it costs you nothing to find out whether my edit suits your content.

What kind of content do you work with?

Podcasts, interviews, talking-head, gameplay, product and lifestyle footage. Not adult content, and nothing I'd have to misrepresent.
